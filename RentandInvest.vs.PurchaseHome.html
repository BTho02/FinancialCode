Assumptions: WI property tax ~1.7%/yr of home value, insurance ~0.35%/yr, 3% closing costs, 7% selling costs, 30-year fixed loan term. Location price/rent defaults and the mortgage-rate table are estimates for illustration, not live market data. Investment returns use actual annual S&P 500 total returns (dividends reinvested) for 1975–2025 as a VOO proxy (VOO launched 2010). Home appreciation uses approximate annual Case-Shiller US National Home Price Index changes for 1975–2025. Rent growth uses approximate annual CPI "rent of primary residence" changes for 1975–2025 (1975–1988 are rough period estimates; 1989–2025 track published BLS/FRED figures). All three are national benchmarks, not Fox Valley-specific. Years outside 1975–2025 use the sliders' assumed rates.
